Use freely

Individuals, researchers and companies can use the software internally, including for profit, without royalties under the license.

Choose how to give back

Standard (CRL-S): deployed or distributed modifications trigger source sharing and upstream submission, including internal production changes.

Distribution (CRL-D): private internal modifications may remain private. Distribution or permitted external offerings of modified substantial functionality trigger reciprocity, even for invitation-only access.

Neither variant requires maintainers to merge submissions. Both preserve commercial restrictions.

Respect the work

Unauthorized commercial resale, white-label exploitation and paid hosted services offering substantial project functionality are restricted. Rights holders may grant separate commercial permissions.
