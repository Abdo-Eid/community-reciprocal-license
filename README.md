# Community Reciprocal License (CRL) v1.0

[![License: CRL v1.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CRL%20v1.0-blue.svg)](CRL-1.0.txt)

**Free to use. Free to improve. Required to give back.**

A proposed source-available software license designed to encourage collective innovation while protecting projects against unauthorized commercial resale and hosted-service exploitation.

> **Status:** Public draft; pending independent legal review. Not OSI-approved and not a registered SPDX license. Publication does not establish legal validity.

## Principles

- Free personal, educational and internal business use, regardless of organization size.
- Free noncommercial forks and redistribution under the same terms.
- Public source sharing and good-faith upstream submission of deployed or distributed modifications.
- No unauthorized sale, commercial SaaS, white-label resale or managed hosting of the covered software or its substantial functionality.
- Copyright and attribution preserved; independently developed works remain outside the license.

Internal commercial use is allowed. Selling the software or offering its substantial functionality commercially is reserved to the relevant rights holders. Independent outputs and genuinely independent consulting are permitted subject to the operative terms.

## Adopt the license

1. Download [CRL-1.0.txt](CRL-1.0.txt) and save it as `LICENSE` in your project's root.
2. Fill in the year, copyright holder's legal name, project name, canonical project repository and licensing/security contact. The canonical repository must be YOUR project's upstream repository, not this license repository.
3. Check third-party license compatibility and obtain legal review.
4. Reference your local LICENSE file from your README. The optional custom SPDX reference is `LicenseRef-CRL-1.0`.
5. If you intend to commercially relicense third-party contributions, consider the separate [draft CLA](CLA.md) before accepting them.

The license text is reusable verbatim with covered works as provided in section 11.5. The project-specific header is a template. This repository is the reference repository for the draft license, not the upstream repository for software adopting it. No blanket license for unrelated repository materials is implied.
