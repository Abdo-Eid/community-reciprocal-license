# Changelog

## CRL-D 1.0 — public draft

- Added Distribution as a separate variant, permitting private internal production modifications without reciprocity solely for internal deployment.
- Retained reciprocity for distribution and permitted external offerings of modified substantial functionality.
- Preserved section 4 commercial restrictions and the original CRL v1.0 text verbatim.
- Added comparison, selection guidance and variant-specific use cases. Both variants remain pending legal review.

## CRL Standard 1.0 — original public draft

- Initial publication of the proposed Community Reciprocal License.
- Free personal and internal commercial use.
- Reciprocal source publication and upstream submission obligations.
- Restrictions on unauthorized commercial distribution and hosted offerings.
- Attribution, security disclosure, patent, termination and liability provisions.
- Pending legal review. No OSI approval or official SPDX registration claimed.

Subsequent changes to operative terms should receive a new version identifier; this draft's terms are not retroactively amended for already licensed versions.
