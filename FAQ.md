# Frequently asked questions

This FAQ summarizes the proposed CRL v1.0. The [complete license](CRL-1.0.txt) controls.

| Activity | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| Personal use and private experiments | Allowed |
| Internal use by commercial companies | Allowed |
| Internal deployment of modifications | Publish corresponding source and submit upstream within 30 days |
| Noncommercial distribution of modifications | Publish source at distribution; submit upstream within 30 days |
| Commercial SaaS or resale | Separate written authorization required |
| Independent commercial outputs | Allowed subject to sections 3 and 4 |
| Independent consulting | Allowed within section 3.2(f) |
| Independently created competing software | Outside CRL |

## Must I merge every improvement?
No. The contributor must make a good-faith submission. Maintainers retain review and rejection rights. Section 5.4 provides an alternative if upstream submission is unavailable.

## Must a company disclose its entire infrastructure?
No. Unrelated proprietary systems, credentials, private datasets and customer information are excluded. Actual covered modifications must still be shared. See sections 2.5 and 5.

## What about security fixes?
Section 5.5 permits narrowly limited coordinated disclosure delays. It does not permit public distribution while withholding corresponding source.

## Can maintainers make money?
Rights holders may separately license material they own or are authorized to relicense. A separate CLA can help obtain those rights. CRL alone does not grant proprietary relicensing rights in contributions owned by others.

## Does this stop AI clones?
AI-assisted changes to copyrighted covered material remain subject to the license where applicable. Independent implementations and rights available under law are not prohibited.

## Is this open source?
It is source-available, not OSI-approved open source. Its commercial restrictions and internal-deployment disclosure obligations are deliberate.

## Is it legally validated?
No. This is a proposed custom license pending independent legal review. Check dependencies, enforceability, jurisdiction and contributor permissions before adoption.
